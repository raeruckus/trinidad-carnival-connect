Cancellation Policy

Your draft package is completely free. After this, if you intend to move forward with us, your down payment is required and it is non-refundable.

Standard Registration Clients

The cancellation period begins after your second monthly installment and carries on until the 1st of December of your registration year. Once no work has been done on your behalf, 100% of your concierge fee (outside of your non-refundable down payment) will be refunded to you.

However, once services have been procured (e.g. fete tickets), it is the client's responsibility to find someone to purchase that service on their behalf (from Trinidad Carnival Connect). If this is done within 15 business days of cancellation, a 100% refund of the service cost (e.g. ticket price) will be provided. No percentage of the concierge fee will be refunded.

After the specified period, cancellation by the client forfeits ownership of all package purchases made on their behalf, making them non-transferable.

Late Registration Clients

For late registration clients, any cancellation results in no refunds, either of concierge fee or service cost.

Pre-Designed Package Cancellations

The cancellation period begins immediately after your registration and continues until 48 hours before your arrival date. Once no work has been done on your behalf, 100% of your concierge fee (outside of your non-refundable down payment) will be refunded to you.

Once work has begun, no amount of the concierge fee already paid will be refunded. If your package has been executed, the entire concierge fee will be taken from the payments you have already made.

Any services acquired from third parties will fall under their refund policies (see Third-Party and Supplier Terms below). If they allow refunds, that money will be returned to you in accordance with the previous paragraph.

All refunds will be issued by Trinidad Carnival Connect to the client via Zelle transfer.

Late Fees

After you have made your down payment, all monthly installments are due on or before the 1st of the following month and will be invoiced as such (or based on the agreement you received based on the package ordered).

If your payment has not cleared by that date, a late fee of $25.00 USD per week (or part thereof) will apply, calculated from the date specified in your payment plan.

If your payment remains outstanding for two weeks, or late payments without notification happen concurrently, Trinidad Carnival Connect retains the right to cancel your package, resulting in the loss of all work done and monies paid.
